PriceTag

Community price tracker

Same item (matched by tag/barcode) across stores, cheapest first. Prices are compared per unit.

Standard seller tag

For prices to be comparable across competing stores, sellers print the same tag format on every item. PriceTag reads two kinds of tags:

  • Standard PriceTag QR — text in the form
    PT1|SKU-or-GTIN|Item name|price|unit|YYYY-MM-DD end|Store
    Fields after the SKU are optional. This is what a participating seller prints.
  • Any UPC/EAN barcode — works today without seller cooperation. The barcode is the comparison key.
